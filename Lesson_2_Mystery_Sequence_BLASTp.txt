EXERCISE: Identify the Mystery Protein

A colleague just sent you this protein sequence isolated
from a human tumor sample. The sample shows abnormal
cell proliferation. She suspects it might be a key
player in cancer biology — but has no idea which protein
it is. Your mission: identify it using BLASTp.

STEP-BY-STEP INSTRUCTIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------

Step 1 — Go to:
   blast.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov

Step 2 — Click:
   "Protein BLAST (BLASTp)"

Step 3 — Paste the mystery sequence into the query box

Step 4 — Settings:
   Database:  Non-redundant protein sequences (nr)
   Organism:  Homo sapiens (taxid: 9606)
   Matrix:    BLOSUM62
   Program:   blastp

Step 5 — Click BLAST and wait ~30 seconds


QUESTIONS TO ANSWER
--------------------------------------------------------------

Q1. What protein did BLAST identify?
    Write the full protein name, gene symbol
    and the organism.

Q2. What is the E-value of the top hit?
    What does this E-value tell you about the match?

Q3. What is the % identity and % query coverage?
    Is this a perfect match or not?

Q4. On which chromosome does this gene reside?
    How many isoforms does this protein have?

Q5. Scroll down the hit list.
    Can you find the same protein in other species?
    Which species appear, and what are
    their % identities compared to human?

Q6. Look at the alignment shown by BLAST.
    Can you identify any mismatches or gaps?
    What could these represent biologically?

Q7. Based on what you find — why do you think
    this protein is one of the most studied
    in cancer biology?
    What is its main function in normal cells?
